Getting started

  1. Click the Sovereign icon in Chrome's toolbar to open it in the side panel. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so it's always one click away.
  2. Read the disclaimer, choose your track and level, and enter a name.
  3. Use the arrow icon at the top to open the roomier full view in a tab.

Back up your data

Everything is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the extension erases it, so use Settings › Export backup regularly. To restore, use Settings › Import backup and choose the saved file.

App lock & privacy

  • Turn on Settings › Privacy & security › Turn on App lock to protect Sovereign with a passcode. Your journal and backups are then encrypted.
  • Choose when it locks: whenever Sovereign is closed, or after 5 or 30 minutes away. Use the lock icon at the top to lock it at once.
  • Forgot your passcode? It can't be recovered, because it's never stored. On the lock screen, choose "Forgot passcode?" to remove the lock. Your encrypted journal is erased, but your practice history stays.
  • Reminder notifications are discreet by default. Change this under Privacy & security.

Reminders

  • Reminders are optional and never more than two a day: your daily practice reminder (Settings) and a focus reminder you can add on Home.
  • They arrive as Chrome notifications while Chrome is running. If you don't see them, check that Chrome notifications are allowed in your device's settings.
  • Turn them all off with Settings › Gentle reminders › Off.

Sound sessions

  • Binaural beats need headphones, with one tone in each ear. Use the "Check L / R" button to test them.
  • Keep the volume low. Don't listen while driving, and avoid sound sessions if you have epilepsy or a history of seizures.

Privacy

Sovereign collects nothing and sends nothing. Read the full privacy policy.

Sovereign is an educational and tracking tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice.
