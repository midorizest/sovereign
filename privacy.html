Sovereign

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 (updated for v1.3) · Applies to the Sovereign Chrome extension and the Sovereign web app.

In short: Sovereign does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers.

What Sovereign stores

To work, Sovereign saves the following only on your device:

  • Your profile: the name you choose, your track, your level, and your optional reminder time.
  • Your practice log: completed practices, minutes, check-ins, cycle phases, streaks, points, and lessons completed.
  • Your journal entries.
  • App preferences such as theme and sound settings.

In the Chrome extension this is stored using Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). In the web app it is stored in your browser's local storage. Some of this information, such as journal entries, cycle phases, and retention check-ins, may be personal or health-related. It never leaves your device.

What Sovereign does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It does not read your browsing history, the websites you visit, or the content of any web page.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • It does not load code from the internet. All of the app's code is included in the package you install.
  • It does not require an account or sign-in.

Permissions the extension uses

  • storage: to save your practice log and journal on your device.
  • alarms: to schedule your optional daily practice reminder.
  • notifications: to show that reminder.
  • sidePanel: to open Sovereign in Chrome's side panel.
  • offscreen: to keep a sound session you started playing after you close the side panel. Sounds are generated on your device; nothing is streamed or downloaded.

Backups, export, and deletion

You can export your data at any time from Settings › Export backup. This creates a file that is saved to your device's Downloads folder; what you do with that file is up to you. You can permanently delete all data from Settings › Reset all data. Uninstalling the extension also deletes its stored data.

Children

Sovereign is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because Sovereign collects no data, any change would be announced here before it takes effect.
